Connect this to your live Google Sheet

  1. In Google Sheets, open the Data tab → File > Share > Publish to web.
  2. Choose the Data sheet, format Comma-separated values (.csv), and publish.
  3. Copy the generated URL and paste it into CONFIG.sheetCsvUrl near the top of the script in this file.
  4. Keep the same column headers: Date, Vendor name, Customer Name, PRODUCT NAME, Qty, Bill Amt (Tax Exclusive), PURCHASE RATE, CUSTOMER INVOICE NO, sales price.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

  1. Commit this file as index.html in your repo.
  2. Enable Pages in Settings > Pages, serving from your default branch.
  3. The page auto‑refreshes from your published sheet every 5 minutes, and the Sync button pulls on demand.
  4. Optional — add columns Payment Status and Due Date to the sheet to unlock paid/pending and aging in the accounts payable section.
